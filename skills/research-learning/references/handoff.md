# Share mode: from learning to writing

Use when the learner wants to write about, present or publish what they learned or ran. This skill does not write the article; it prepares **real material** for the writing chain.

## The chain

```
research-learning  ->  content-topic-decomposer  ->  human-in-the-loop-writing  ->  content-card-designer
 learn and test         plan what to post            write it with you in it        make the cards
```

Each step is optional. Suggest the next skill; do not force it.

## What to hand over

| From this skill | Becomes | For |
|---|---|---|
| An experiment report (real results) | A fact sheet: goal, setup, numbers, what went wrong, what you decided, the moment you remember | content-topic-decomposer's "hero material", so case and story slots hold real experience |
| A concept note (the learner's own understanding, with claim statuses) | The learner's claim, boundary and what they learned | human-in-the-loop-writing's judgment capture |
| A claims ledger | Which statements are paper, derived, checked, conjecture | The writing step, so unverified claims are not published as facts |
| A comparison, a sequence or a number the learner produced | Card content | content-card-designer (numbers must come from the learner's runs) |

## Rules

- **Only real material.** Hand over results that were actually run and conclusions the learner actually holds. Do not turn a plan into a result.
- **Carry the statuses forward.** A claim marked conjecture stays a conjecture in the article.
- **Keep the learner's words.** Their predictions, their wrong turns and their reasoning are the best story material; keep them.
- **Do not publish for them.** Produce the sheet; leave writing and posting to the other skills and the learner.
